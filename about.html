02 — What we believe

The values.

01

Cinema first.

Every project is treated as a film, not a deliverable. We plan, shoot and edit with the same care as a theatrical release.

02

No outsourcing.

Every decision — from casting to color grade — happens inside the bakery. We don't outsource taste.

03

The frame earns.

If a shot doesn't move the story forward, it doesn't make the cut. Every pixel serves a purpose.

04

Light first.

We start with light. Everything else — product, brand, message — comes after we've found the right light.

04 — Journey

The timeline.

2018

Bakery Films founded

Three producers, one kitchen, zero compromise. Started in a small office in Mumbai with a single promise: every film matters.

2020

First international commission

Expanded to worldwide production with a brand film shot across three continents. The oven went global.

2022

Director roster launches

Onboarded 10 directors with distinct visual languages. From documentary to fashion, every genre covered.

2024

100th film delivered

Milestone reached. A century of frames, stories and brands brought to life. The kitchen never closes.

2026

The archive goes live

This site. A living portfolio of everything we've made, and everything we're about to make.
