Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 2026 · Mumbai, India

1. What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form, we receive your name, email address, and anything you include in your message (company, timeline, budget range, project details). We also receive standard technical data your browser shares automatically (IP address, device and browser type, pages visited).

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss commissions.
  • To improve the site (aggregated, anonymous analytics).
  • We never sell your personal data. We share it only where required by law or with service providers who help us operate the site (e.g. hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality.

3. Cookies

We use a single consent cookie to remember your cookie choice, and privacy-friendly analytics cookies only after you accept. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser storage — the banner will reappear.

4. Data retention

Enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months for business records, then deleted. Analytics data is aggregated and retained for up to 14 months.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to info@bakeryfilms.co.in. We respond within 30 days.
